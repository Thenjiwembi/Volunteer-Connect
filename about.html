Our story

Good people are
everywhere.

Volunteer Connect exists to make the distance between wanting to help and actually helping feel a little smaller.

The why

A better way to show up.

We believe volunteering should feel less like finding a form and more like finding your people. There are community organizations doing thoughtful, urgent work every day. There are people with time, energy, and care to give. The connection between them should be clear.

Volunteer Connect helps people find organizations where they can volunteer, helps organizations welcome the right support, and helps everyone see the difference their time makes.
