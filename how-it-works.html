Good questions

Clarity is part
of the care.

Do I need experience to volunteer?

Usually not. Organisations describe what they need, and you can choose opportunities that match your experience, interests, and capacity.

How do I know an organisation is legitimate?

Organisations go through a review process before their public opportunities are published. We also encourage clear, respectful communication throughout.
